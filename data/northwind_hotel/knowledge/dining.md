# Food and drink

## Breakfast
Breakfast is served in the Tidewater Room from 7:00 to 10:30 am on weekdays and 7:30 to 11:00 am at weekends. It is included in every room rate. Gluten-free bread and plant-based milk are always available.

## Restaurant
The Tidewater Room serves dinner from 6:00 to 10:00 pm, Tuesday to Sunday. It is closed on Mondays. Main courses cost €18 to €34. Reservations are recommended at weekends.

## Bar
The Lantern Bar is open from 4:00 pm to midnight every day and serves small plates until 10:00 pm.

## Room service
Room service runs from 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, with a €5 tray charge per order.
