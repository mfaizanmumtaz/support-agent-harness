# Policies

## Cancellation
Flexible rates can be cancelled free of charge until 48 hours before arrival. After that, the first night is charged. Saver rates are 15% cheaper but non-refundable.

## Refunds
Refunds for cancelled flexible bookings are returned to the original card within 5 to 10 working days. Requests for a refund after a stay, for example because of a problem with the room, are reviewed by our guest relations team.

## Pets
Dogs are welcome in Classic Double and Sea View Double rooms for €15 per night, with a limit of one dog per room. Other pets are not allowed. Assistance dogs stay free in any room.

## Smoking
All rooms and indoor areas are non-smoking. Smoking in a room carries a €150 cleaning fee. The harbour terrace has a smoking area.

## Children
Children of all ages are welcome. Children under 6 stay free in their parents' room, breakfast included.

## Group bookings
Bookings of more than 10 rooms are handled by our events team, who can offer group rates and a private breakfast room.

## Lost property
Items left behind are kept for 60 days. We can post them at cost; ask our guest relations team.
