# Rooms and rates

## Room types
- **Harbour Single**: one single bed, 16 m², from €89 per night.
- **Classic Double**: one queen bed, 22 m², from €129 per night.
- **Sea View Double**: one king bed and a balcony facing the bay, 26 m², from €169 per night.
- **Family Suite**: one king bed and two single beds in a separate room, 40 m², sleeps four, from €239 per night.

Rates include breakfast. Prices rise by about 20% in July and August.

## Extra beds and cots
A baby cot is free on request. An extra bed for a child under 12 costs €20 per night and fits only in Sea View Doubles and Family Suites.

## Accessibility
Four Classic Double rooms on the ground floor are wheelchair accessible, with roll-in showers and grab rails. The lift reaches every floor.
