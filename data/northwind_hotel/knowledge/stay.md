# Your stay at Northwind Hotel

Northwind Hotel is a 64-room hotel in the old harbour district of Halden Bay, a fictional seaside town.

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in starts at 3:00 pm and check-out is by 11:00 am. Early check-in from 12:00 pm is possible for €25 when a room is ready; ask at the front desk on the day. Late check-out until 2:00 pm costs €30 and must be booked the day before.

## Front desk
The front desk is staffed 24 hours a day. Guests arriving after midnight receive a door code by text message on the day of arrival.

## Luggage
We store luggage free of charge before check-in and after check-out, until 10:00 pm on the day of departure.

## Parking
Our underground garage has 20 spaces at €18 per night. Spaces can't be reserved in advance. Two of them have electric-vehicle chargers, charged at €0.40 per kWh.

## Wi-Fi
Wi-Fi is free in all rooms and public areas. The network is "Northwind-Guest" and the password is printed on your key card sleeve.
